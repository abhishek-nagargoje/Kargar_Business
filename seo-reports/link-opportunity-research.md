## 2. Business Association Deep-Dive

### MCCIA — Mahratta Chamber of Commerce, Industries & Agriculture
- **Official site:** mcciapune.com
- **Real:** Founded 1934, 3,000+ members, one of India's oldest chambers. Runs a "Digital Directory" for members (login-gated, email/membership-number access) and publishes a printed/digital "Industrial & Commercial Directory" (10,500+ manufacturing/service-sector contacts).
- **Membership eligibility:** Open, general-business chamber — Kargar (an active Pune facility-services company) would very likely qualify as a standard member; no FM-specific eligibility barrier found.
- **Link opportunity, honestly assessed:** A member's digital directory listing is real and editorially legitimate (not a spam directory — it's a paid-membership body's own member database), but it is **login-gated**, not a public, crawlable backlink in the traditional SEO sense. The real value here is legitimacy/networking, with a directory listing as a secondary benefit — not primarily a link-building play.
- **Recommended path:** Contact MCCIA's Membership Department (02025709168, per public listing) to ask about membership process and whether the digital directory listing is public-facing or member-only.

### DCCIA Pune — Dalit Indian Chamber of Commerce & Industry, Pune chapter
- **Official site:** dcciapune.org (has a `/membership/` page)
- **Real:** ~180 members, active industrial-belt chamber (Vadgaon Sheri, Pune HQ), runs conclaves/recognition events.
- **Eligibility:** Search results didn't return explicit eligibility criteria beyond a membership application page existing — genuine uncertainty, not fabricated detail.
- **Recommended path:** Review dcciapune.org/membership/ directly and/or contact the chamber (address found: 305 Gulmohar Centre Point, Vadgaon Sheri, Pune) to confirm eligibility and any public member-listing component.

### PMA — Pune Management Association
- **Official site:** punemanagementassociation.org
- **Real:** Established 1977, 1,200+ senior members, general management-professional body (not FM-specific), runs training/events.
- **Relevance assessment:** Lower topical fit than MCCIA/DCCIA (management-education focus, not business/industry membership), but genuine and real.
- **Recommended path:** Lower priority than MCCIA/DCCIA given topical fit; contact via Training@PuneManagementAssociation.org if pursued.

### New finding — CREDAI-Pune Metro (real estate developers' association)
- **Official site:** credaipune.org
- **Real:** Pioneer developer/promoter association in Pune since 1982, 440+ developer members (~70% of Pune construction activity), 17 expert committees.
- **Why this matters more than PMA for Kargar specifically:** Kargar's strongest verified relationships (§1) are almost entirely CREDAI-member real-estate developers (Godrej, Kolte-Patil, Kumar Properties, Rohan Builders, Sukhwani, Majestique, Supreme Universal). CREDAI itself is a developer association, not open to FM vendors as primary members — but it sometimes runs "associate member" or vendor-empanelment categories at local-chapter level. This was **not confirmed** in this search (no explicit "facility management associate member" category found) — flagged as worth a direct inquiry, not assumed to exist.
- **Recommended path:** Contact CREDAI-Pune directly (credaipune.org) to ask whether an associate/vendor membership category exists for facility-management service providers, given Kargar already serves multiple CREDAI-member developers.

- No specific "ISHM/ISSA India" facility-management/housekeeping association was found with a confirmed Pune presence — search returned only unrelated housekeeping *service providers* and an unrelated hospitality-management college (ISHM Kolkata).
