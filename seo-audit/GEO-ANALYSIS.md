# GEO / AI Search Analysis — kargarbusinessservices.com

**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Scope:** the 41 sitemap URLs, measured from the prerendered production build (`frontend/dist`) plus live checks against `https://www.kargarbusinessservices.com`.

**Framing:** Google's position is that optimizing for AI search **is still SEO**. The findings below are SEO fundamentals applied to AI surfaces (Google AI Overviews / AI Mode, ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Bing Copilot), not a separate discipline.

---

## 1. GEO Readiness Score: **61 / 100**

| Criterion | Weight | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Citability | 25 | 15 | Service pages open with a solid self-contained "KARGAR is…" paragraph. The homepage opens with generic copy. The site has no unique data, and its headline stats contradict each other (see §6). |
| Structural readability | 20 | 15 | Every page has exactly one H1, a clean H2 hierarchy, tables on 22 of 41 pages and FAQ blocks. Only 15 of 41 pages use a question-form H2/H3. |
| Multi-modal | 15 | 6 | Pages have images but no video, diagrams or calculators. Every guide uses the logo as its `Article.image`. |
| Authority & brand | 20 | 7 | Dates appear only on the 4 guides. Authorship is the Organization, with no named person. There is no founder or `foundingDate` in schema, and almost no off-site footprint (see §5). |
| Technical accessibility | 20 | 18 | All pages are prerendered to static HTML, robots.txt allows all crawlers, and the JSON-LD `@graph` is consistent. There is no llms.txt, which costs nothing under this rubric. |

## 3. AI Crawler Access: ✅ All allowed

The live `robots.txt` reads `User-agent: * / Allow: / / Disallow: /admin` plus the sitemap line. I verified the live responses:

| UA | `/housekeeping-services-pune` |
|---|---|
| Browser | 200, 72,298 B |
| GPTBot/1.1 | 200, 72,298 B (identical) |
| ClaudeBot/1.0 | 200, 72,298 B (identical) |
| PerplexityBot/1.0 | 200, 72,298 B (identical) |

There is no UA-based blocking or cloaking. Training crawlers (CCBot, Google-Extended, Bytespider) are also allowed, which is a business choice rather than a defect. If you want to opt out of training while keeping search visibility, add explicit `Disallow` groups for those three only. robots.txt is generated by `frontend/scripts/seo/generate-robots.ts`, so edit it there.

## 4. llms.txt: ❌ Missing (live returns 404)

Google Search ignores llms.txt. It neither helps nor harms Google visibility or AI Overviews, and there is no evidence it is a citation lever for other major AI search systems. It is optional and low priority. If you want it for non-Google tools, use this template:

```
# KARGAR Facility Management
> Women-led housekeeping, security and integrated facility management company in Baner, Pune (Kargar Business Services Pvt. Ltd.), serving Pune and PCMC.

## Services
- [Housekeeping services in Pune](https://www.kargarbusinessservices.com/housekeeping-services-pune): Managed, supervised housekeeping for offices, societies, schools, healthcare and plants
```

## 6. Passage-Level Citability

**Good (keep):**
- `/housekeeping-services-pune`, 2nd paragraph: "KARGAR Facility Management (Kargar Business Services Pvt. Ltd.) is a housekeeping and integrated facility management company based in Baner, Pune. We provide housekeeping as a managed service…" This is a definitional, self-contained block about 120 words long, placed in the top 30% of the page. It is the model to copy.
- `/resources/housekeeping-cost-pune`: a cost-drivers table plus an honest "we deliberately do not publish rates" stance. Both are quotable.

**Problems:**
1. **Contradictory headline stats.** The homepage and meta description claim "10,000+ Happy Clients" alongside "50+ Sites Managed" (`src/pages/KargarSinglePage.tsx:91-92`, `src/features/seo/registry.ts:60`, `CompanyStatsList.tsx:6`, `ReviewsHeader.tsx:5`). AI systems and human evaluators both read 10,000 clients across 50 sites as implausible, and a figure that looks inflated lowers trust in every other claim on the site. **Confirm the real numbers with the business before changing them.** If "10,000+" means workers or people served, relabel it.
2. **The homepage has no definition in its first 60 words.** It opens with "Reliable Facility Management Solutions that keep your Business Running Seamlessly", which is generic and could belong to any company.
3. **Award claims carry no source link.** "Recognized by the Hon'ble Prime Minister" with no citation is hard for an AI system to verify, so it is unlikely to be repeated.

## 7. Server-Side Rendering Check: ✅ Pass

- `npm run build` runs `scripts/seo/prerender.ts`, which emits a static `.html` file per route. `vercel.json` rewrites clean URLs to those files.
- Word counts in the raw HTML, before any JavaScript runs: service pages 533–1,727 words, guides 604–867, homepage 1,059. AI crawlers see the full content.
- All JSON-LD is in the static HTML.
- **Thin pages:** `/support` (203 words), `/sectors` (283), `/hard-services` (331), `/soft-services` (337), `/contact-us` (341), `/company-profile` (349). `/company-profile` should be the canonical "about the entity" page, so it is the most important of these to expand.

## 9. Schema Recommendations

Already strong: a single `@graph` with `Organization`, `LocalBusiness` (`parentOrganization`), `WebSite`, `BreadcrumbList`, `Service` (`provider` → org), `Article` and `FAQPage`. All of it parses.

Add:
- **Organization:** `foundingDate`, `founder` (`Person` with name, `jobTitle`, `sameAs` → their LinkedIn), `numberOfEmployees`, `subjectOf` (press articles), `hasMap`/GBP URL in `sameAs`, and YouTube in `sameAs` once it exists.
- **Article (guides):** replace the logo `image` with a real topic image (1200 px or wider). Keep `author` as the Organization unless a real, consenting named author exists. `guides.ts` correctly forbids invented people.
- **WebPage `dateModified`** on service pages.
- **VideoObject** once videos exist.
- Keep FAQPage. Google only shows FAQ rich results for authoritative government and health sites, but the markup costs nothing and the visible Q&A is what AI systems actually use.

## 10. Content Reformatting Suggestions

**Homepage hero subtitle.** Make the definition the first thing a reader sees:
> KARGAR Facility Management is a 100% women-led housekeeping, security and facility management company in Baner, Pune. We deploy trained, supervised staff to offices, housing societies, schools, hospitals and plants across Pune and PCMC.

**Turn label H2s into question H2s on service pages.** Most service pages have 0 question headings. Rename the H2s that already answer a question, for example:
these real H2s from `/office-housekeeping-services-pune`:
- "A Typical Office Housekeeping Day" → "What does a typical office housekeeping day include?"
- "Roles We Provide for Offices" → "Which housekeeping roles does an office need?"
- "Sizing Housekeeping for a Smaller Office" → "How many housekeeping staff does a small office need?" Link this one to the staffing guide.

Keep one sentence that directly answers each question right after the heading, then put the detail below it.

**Award sentence.** Add the source:
> On 20 September 2024 in Wardha, KARGAR was recognised by the Prime Minister of India as the first 100% women-led company in its sector [link to coverage]. It also received the Punyashlok Ahilya Devi Holkar Woman Startup Award (2024) from the Government of Maharashtra.

**Thin hub pages** (`/hard-services`, `/soft-services`, `/sectors`): add a 134–167 word "What are hard services?" style definition block at the top of each.
