# P2 Rejected Opportunities

Everything below was investigated and deliberately excluded from `p2-opportunities.md`/`p2-prospects.csv` — listed here so the exclusion is a documented decision, not a silent omission padded over with unrelated filler.

| Opportunity | Reason for rejection |
|---|---|
| Facilities Expo (facilitiesexpo.com) | Confirmed US-based (California/Oregon/Texas/Nevada), 40-year-old FM trade show — geographically irrelevant to a Pune-only commercial target; no realistic business case for a Pune housekeeping company to exhibit there |
| ISSA (International Sanitary Supply Association) | Real, legitimate global cleaning-industry association — but no confirmed India-specific chapter, membership program, or Pune presence was found in this research pass. Not rejected as illegitimate, just not currently actionable without further verification of an India entry point |
| clipstrust.com "Best business listing websites in India" / findlocalvendors.com "Top 5 business directories" | These are SEO-listicle/blog articles *about* directories, not directories themselves, and read as generic affiliate-style content farms — excluded as noise, not a real opportunity |
| Generic "Top 50 business listing sites" documents (e.g. Scribd uploads) | Unverifiable, undifferentiated bulk-directory lists — exactly the "hundreds of low-quality directory links" pattern explicitly forbidden by the master brief's Phase 0 rules. Rejected outright, not partially used |
| Any directory requiring payment solely for a dofollow link | None was specifically identified as demanding this in this research pass, but the rule is stated here for completeness: if any directory's paid tier is sold explicitly as "pay for a followed backlink" rather than a real business/marketing service, it is rejected regardless of authority |
| Casino/adult/crypto/gambling/spam sites | None surfaced in this research (as expected, given the search terms used) — noted as a completed check, not a finding |
| Foreign directories with no India/Pune relevance | None specifically pursued in this pass beyond the Facilities Expo case above; general principle applied per Phase 0 rule 12 |
| Generic GMB/local-SEO agency websites (digitalentire.in, thestorycart.com, advertiseasy.com, serviceninjas.in, etc.) | These are third-party marketing agencies selling GBP-optimization *services*, not citation or link opportunities themselves — appeared only as search noise while researching Google Business Profile, not a real prospect |
| Pune Realty (punerealty.in) — held to P3, not fully rejected | Kept in `p2-prospects.csv` at P3 with an explicit "low-confidence fit" flag rather than fully rejected, since it's a real, RERA-registered Pune platform — but it primarily serves property developers/buyers, not FM vendors, and no evidence was found that it accepts vendor-side editorial content. Do not pursue without first directly confirming fit. |

## What was explicitly NOT done, per the master brief's non-negotiable rules

- No automated or bulk directory submission was performed or scripted.
- No paid link placements were investigated as a purchase option.
- No PBN, link-farm, or comment/forum-spam sources were sought out (none appeared incidentally either).
- No fake company profile, fake review, or fabricated relationship was created or proposed.
