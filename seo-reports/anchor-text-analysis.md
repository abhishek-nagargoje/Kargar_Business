## 2. Anchor Strategy for New Links (Phase 4)

Do **not** use "housekeeping services Pune" as the anchor for every new link — Phase 4 of the brief explicitly forbids this, and it is also a real over-optimization risk once real links start accumulating.

Target mix for links pointed at the housekeeping page specifically (guideline, not a quota to force):

| Type | Example anchors | Target share |
|---|---|---|
| Brand | "Kargar", "Kargar Business Services", "Kargar Facility Management" | 30–40% |
| Branded + keyword | "Kargar housekeeping services", "Kargar facility management" | 10–15% |
| Naked URL / "this page" | `kargarbusinessservices.com`, "this facility management provider", "company website" | 10–20% |
| Generic | "learn more", "their housekeeping page", "read more here" | 10–15% |
| Partial-match keyword | "housekeeping services in Pune", "professional housekeeping services", "commercial housekeeping" | 10–15% |
| Exact-match keyword | "housekeeping services Pune" | ≤5–8%, only where it reads naturally in the linking sentence |
| Long-tail / descriptive | "corporate housekeeping services for offices in Pune", "industrial housekeeping standards" | 5–10% |

**Rule of thumb:** the anchor text should be whatever the linking author would naturally write about Kargar in that specific sentence — never dictate exact anchor text to a source in outreach copy (see `outreach-templates.md`).

## 3. Anchor by Link-Target Page (Phase 16)

Not every link should point at the housekeeping page:

- **Homepage:** brand-only anchors ("Kargar", "Kargar Business Services", naked URL)
- **`/services/soft-services` (soft services hub):** "soft services", "housekeeping and soft facility services"
- **`/services/soft-services/housekeeping` (primary target):** the mix in §2
- **Relevant industry/sector content (once built):** contextual, topic-specific anchors, not commercial keyword anchors

This avoids the single-page anchor concentration Phase 16 warns against.
