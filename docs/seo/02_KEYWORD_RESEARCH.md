Localities appear in context instead: Baner (office), western/eastern business districts and PCMC on the pillars, and Chakan/Talegaon/Bhosari/PCMC MIDC on the industrial security page. **Revisit** only if the business can document real, distinct operations in a locality (for example a supervised cluster of sites in Chakan with local supervisors).
